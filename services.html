What We Offer

From dog rescue and veterinary care to foster homes and adoption, our animal shelter supports every animal from intake to its new home. Select a service to read more.

Responding to calls for abandoned or stray animals.

Providing vaccinations, sterilisations, and life-saving surgeries.

Helping traumatised animals rebuild trust with humans.

Placing animals into vetted homes for one-on-one care.

Matching our animals with responsible and loving owners. Meet the animals waiting for a home.

Offering ongoing support and training advice to adopters.

Visiting schools to teach children about animal empathy and care.

Sharing resources and food with smaller rescue groups.

Featured: Wesbank Outreach Programme

Taking our services directly to areas with limited access to animal care. We provide:

  • Nutritious pet food.
  • Tick, flea, and mange treatments.
  • Basic wound care.
  • Transport for sterilisations.
