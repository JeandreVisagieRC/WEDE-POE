Who We Are

We are a registered non-profit organization in the Western Cape, dedicated to rescuing and rehabilitating domestic animals.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide immediate rescue and care for animals in distress.

Vision: A community where every animal is treated with compassion and has a safe home.

Our Founding Story

We started in 2010 when a small group of friends began feeding stray dogs out of the back of a bakkie. Today, we run a full rehabilitation center.

Meet the Team

  • Sarah Jenkins (Founder): Oversees daily operations with 15 years of experience.
  • Dr. Thabo Mokoena (Head Vet): Ensures all intakes receive prompt medical care.
  • Lisa Van Der Merwe (Adoptions): Matches rescues with their perfect families.

Our Goals

We are working towards expanding our on-site clinic and doubling our community outreach efforts.
