Animal Rescue in Cape Town: Give a Rescue a Second Chance

Grace Animal Sanctuary is an animal rescue organisation in Cape Town. We rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned dogs and cats, because every animal deserves a loving, safe and permanent home.

See our dogs for adoption

What We Do

Rescue

We save abandoned and abused animals from vulnerable situations.

Rehabilitate

We provide veterinary care and behavioral training to help animals heal.

Rehome

We match our healthy, happy rescues with loving forever families.

Wesbank Community Outreach

We provide vital food and basic veterinary care to the Wesbank community every month.
